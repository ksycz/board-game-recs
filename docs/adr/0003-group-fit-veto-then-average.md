# Group Fit: any dislike excludes, then average

In Play mode the Recommendation must suit the whole Table, not only the asking Member. A Game that any Member at the Table dislikes is excluded outright; among the remaining Games we rank by the average of the Members' Fits. We chose a veto over a pure average because real groups decide this way ("not Catan again, Petra hates it") and because a high average can hide one miserable Player. Players who are not Members count only towards the player count; quick guest questions were deferred.
