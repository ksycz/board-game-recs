# AI behind one swappable tagger, under a budget the Admin controls

All AI calls go through a single tagger module (Claude API today), so the provider can be swapped without touching the rest of the code. The Admin funds the AI with a small prepaid credit and is willing to top it up, but never without knowing. So the app keeps its own running total of AI spend against a budget the Admin sets: it emails the Admin at 50% and 80% of the budget, and at 100% it stops calling the AI and emails the Admin again. The Admin raises the budget in the app after topping up the credit; nothing is ever spent beyond the budget without her action. Auto-reload on the provider account stays off as a second safeguard.

When the AI is unavailable or the budget is reached, Members pick Tags by hand and Unmatched Phrases simply wait. To spend credit only where it matters, the initial Catalogue drafts are produced during development (not by the running app), results are cached, and only anonymised Reason text is ever sent.
